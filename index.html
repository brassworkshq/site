BRASSWORKS

Short science explainers — drawn from the numbers, not around them.

One idea per video, built from published measurements: why our universe is the size a black hole of its mass would be, how galaxies had already made oxygen when the universe was three hundred million years old, why two good ways of measuring the expansion of the universe give two different answers. Every figure is geometry derived from the source data, and every number is checked against that source before a script is written.

About the videos

Every frame is vector geometry, drawn by code from the published figures it depicts and rendered deterministically — there is no stock footage and no generated imagery. The videos carry no audio. Sources are named on screen or in the first comment. Nothing published here is financial, legal or medical advice, and no video recommends a product, a provider or a course of action.
